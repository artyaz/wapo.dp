```
### Design System Audit
- **Monochrome first:** Follows warm neutral gray palette throughout dark surface tiers. Semantic red is strictly constrained to the "Squat target" validation error state and icon.
- **Three type roles:** Source Serif 4 is applied strictly to the page-level reading heading (`Profile & training settings`), Inter sans-serif handles all UI labels, form controls, and body prose, and IBM Plex Mono is reserved for version badges (`v3.2.0`), timestamps (`09:41`), and numeric metrics.
- **Geometry & Spacing:** Consistent ~8px card radii and ~3px field/button radii with sharp 1px borders. Field controls, two-column splits (`Body weight` / `Height`, `Rest timer` / `Units`), and internal padding follow clean 4px grid increments.
- **Material honesty:** Cards and form containers use flat surfaces with 1px hairlines. Zero improper cast shadows or faux glassmorphism on in-flow elements.
- **Hierarchy & Legibility:** Hierarchy is communicated cleanly via font weight, opacity, and positioning rather than accent hues. Text contrast meets dark theme accessibility requirements across primary, secondary, and disabled/helper text layers.
```
